# Camog pricing

All prices in Australian dollars (AUD), billed annually per licence. Every licence starts with a 14-day free trial: full function, no card required. After the trial Camog stays usable in read-only mode, so records are never locked away.

## Solo

- Price: A$179/year
- Seats: 1 device seat
- For: solo practitioners

## Practice (recommended)

- Price: A$399/year
- Seats: 3 device seats
- For: small practices and specialists

## Clinic

- Price: A$799/year
- Seats: 10 device seats
- For: multi-computer clinics

## Included in every tier

- Phone-as-camera capture, paired to the clinic computer by QR code
- Body map placement and lesion series over time
- Recorded consent with scope (care, education or research) and expiry warnings
- Append-only audit log with CSV export
- AES-256 encryption at rest on the clinic's own computer, no cloud storage
- Full offline operation: capture, review and comparison
- One-click A4 PDF case reports
