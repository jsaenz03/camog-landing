Camog FAQ

Do my photos ever leave the clinic computer?

No. Photos are stored as AES-256 encrypted files on your own machine and never uploaded. The only network traffic Camog generates is licence activation, which carries a device identifier and nothing else.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

Camog keeps working in read-only mode. You can browse, compare and export, but new captures need a licence. Your records are never held hostage.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Capture, review, compare and case reports all work with no internet connection. Activation is the one moment Camog needs to reach the licence server.

Windows or Mac?

Both. Windows 10 and 11 install straight from the Microsoft Store, with updates handled by the Store. macOS builds for Apple Silicon and Intel are available as a direct download.

Can I get photos out of Camog?

Any time. Photos export as JPEGs, case reports as PDFs, and the audit log as CSV. If you ever leave, your data leaves with you.

How does this compare on price?

Purpose-built clinical photography subscriptions commonly run to thousands of dollars per clinician per year. Camog Practice is A$399 a year for three seats, about A$133 per seat.
